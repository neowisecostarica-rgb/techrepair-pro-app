# TRP MB3 — SHINNEN naming candidate

Date: 2026-09-16
Status: ACTIVE CANDIDATE — preliminary clearance only

## Candidate
**SHINNEN**
Japanese: 信念
Working meaning: conviction; firm belief/principle.

## Strategic fit
SHINNEN moves the brand territory away from generic repair software and away from the overused warrior/samurai naming pattern. It retains Japanese cultural depth while communicating conviction, discipline, consistency and principle — qualities compatible with a service-operations platform built around traceability and control.

The name can support the existing product positioning:
- system of record from reception through delivery/payment;
- disciplined operational execution;
- traceability rather than improvisation;
- one connected service history;
- calm, premium authority rather than aggressive/militaristic branding.

## Preliminary public-web collision scan
Searches performed for SHINNEN combined with software, SaaS, app, technology and trademark/software terms did not surface an obvious current service-operations SaaS competitor using the exact SHINNEN word mark in the same commercial territory.

Important caveats:
- This is not legal trademark clearance.
- Search results are not a substitute for WIPO, USPTO, Costa Rica registry and target-market class searches.
- A historical Android developer identity called Shinnen Studio appears in public app-index data; one indexed utility app was removed from Google Play in 2022. This is a digital-name collision to document, but the preliminary evidence does not establish a current competing operations SaaS.
- SHINNEN is an ordinary Japanese word and appears in unrelated cultural/personal contexts, so inherent exclusivity must be evaluated by counsel and by jurisdiction.

## Comparison with current explored names
MAXIMUS: materially crowded/conflicted in technology/software.
RONIN: strong concept but heavily used in technology.
VICIUS: distinctive but has technology/software usage and English-language semantic concerns.
BUJIN: survives preliminary exploration but has existing technology-company/name occurrences and martial associations.
BUSHIN: existing consulting/technology-adjacent usage plus martial-art trademark history; weaker clearance profile.
ISAMU: established Japanese personal name and unrelated marks; less direct product meaning.
SHINNEN: currently the most conceptually differentiated candidate in this exploration, pending formal clearance.

## Brand direction if SHINNEN survives clearance
Master brand: SHINNEN
Product descriptor: Service Operations Platform / Sistema operativo para negocios de servicio técnico
Core narrative: conviction translated into operational discipline.
Avoid pseudo-Japanese visual clichés, samurai helmets, swords, rising-sun motifs, anime treatment, and claims that the word means something it does not.

## Next gate
Before approval as final name:
1. exact-match and confusingly-similar trademark search in relevant Nice classes, especially software/SaaS/business services;
2. WIPO + USPTO + Costa Rica + priority expansion markets;
3. domain and social-handle audit;

5. Spanish/English pronunciation and spelling test;
